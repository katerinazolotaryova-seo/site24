### Link building: what it is

Links — and promoting a site through them — are one of the key components of SEO and an important factor affecting a site's online visibility. Promoting a site through links is often called external optimisation or off-page SEO. It is a broad set of activities aimed at getting links from other sites to the project you are promoting.

In this article we will cover:

- Why links matter for SEO promotion?
- How do SEO specialists acquire links to the sites they promote?
- What types of links exist?
- Should you buy links, and how to do it correctly to get results — not Google penalties?
- How to build a link profile properly?
- How to evaluate the effectiveness of backlink promotion?

### Why do links matter in SEO promotion?

Google does not always say so directly, but links are among the top-3 factors affecting a site's position in search results. This is confirmed by Brian Dean, one of the SEO gurus, in an article on the Backlinko blog.

Why will links always matter for ranking and high positions in Google? There are several reasons:

1. Backlinks from authoritative, high-quality sites signal to Google that the resource is informative and useful and should be shown as high as possible in results.
2. Backlinks pointing to the site speed up its indexing by search engines. Thanks to links, crawlers reach your site faster and scan it. That means Google will include the pages in results sooner.
3. Links from topical sites are an additional signal of which queries to rank the site for.
4. Links can bring your site additional referral visits. There may not be many, but each such visitor tends to be highly interested. They spend time on the site and can even become a customer.

There is also a flip side. Long gone are the days when it was enough to quickly buy as many links as possible, without selecting platforms or placement methods. Google has become more complex and more clever.

We will talk further about how to acquire and where to buy links for site promotion in a way that delivers results.

### How do SEO specialists work with links?

The work of SEO specialists on links is not limited to deciding how and where to buy links for site promotion. It also includes:

- developing a link-building strategy that defines the intensity of the work, the types of links, and the priority pages for link building;
- direct link building — through purchase or other methods;
- analysing the results of external promotion (growth or decline in Google positions, traffic changes);
- adjusting the link-building strategy.

We will go into all of this in more detail, starting with the types of links that exist, how they differ, and how each can be acquired.

### Types of links and how to acquire them

There are several different types of links you can use for site promotion. We will cover the main categories and the ways to acquire each.

#### Article links

These are links placed inside articles published by online media, blogs, and in some cases the sites of other companies. Article links can point to the home page, service pages, product categories, or other articles in your blog.

The choice depends on the specifics of the business and your strategy.

There are several ways to acquire article links to the site being promoted.

##### Exchanges: how to buy links for site promotion correctly?

Link exchanges are online services that act as intermediaries between sites that sell article-placement services with links and those who buy article links.

These services are popular for several reasons:

- they let you choose from a large number of sites across different topics — the catalogues of the most popular exchanges contain thousands or even tens of thousands of platforms;
- all sites can be filtered by several criteria, including placement cost, region, topic, languages, and the site's SEO metrics;
- exchanges simplify managing the budget for link acquisition;
- the exchange administration often acts as an arbiter in disputed situations, guarantees placement quality, and so on.

There are several popular link exchanges.

##### Collaborator.pro

The exchange provides placement opportunities on thousands of sites from dozens of countries. It is suitable for projects in various niches and topics. For each platform, you can review its SEO metrics from services such as Ahrefs and Serpstat.

You can also book advertising in Telegram channels and chats via this service. That option goes beyond SEO, but it can be an additional tool for promoting your site.

##### PRposting

A similar exchange in terms of capabilities. It also offers a wide selection of platforms with the ability to filter by topic, region, language, SEO metrics, and so on.

##### PRnews.io

A service for article-based link building and PR publications. It has comparable capabilities to the two platforms above.

You now know where to buy article links to a site for promotion. But how do you do it correctly? There are several basic recommendations:

- Build links gradually and consistently. Do not expect that one or two expensive article links will lock your site at the top of results forever.
- Identify priority pages for promotion. You will not be able to grow links to every page with equal intensity. So it is important to identify the pages that will bring the most profit over time. Prioritise link building for those.
- Fix technical issues. No ranking factor works at 100% on its own. To get the maximum effect from link building, run a site audit and fix every problem that hinders indexation or lowers click-through in search results.
- Analyse competitors. It is important to determine which platforms they obtain links from, how actively they grow their link mass, and so on. This information helps build the right link-building strategy and estimate the budget needed for results.
- Combine article links with other types of backlinks.

#### Catalogues and directories

This is another type of link worth using in site promotion. Catalogues are resources that gather sites by topic and region. A link is usually accompanied by a brief description of the resource and sometimes contact details.

Catalogues can be general or specialised. They are used less frequently in our segment of the internet, but for promoting English-language resources such sites are still useful. While links from them play a supporting role, we recommend using catalogue links to "dilute" the link profile and improve the SEO metrics of your site.

#### Profile links

There are many resources where users can register, create a profile, and place an active link to their site inside it. Such links are also actively used in link-based promotion. They round out the link profile and make it look more natural.

#### Crowd marketing: links from forums and comments

This is another important category of links. It covers links placed in blog comments, news, and forums.

Platforms for crowd marketing should be chosen carefully. It is better if they meet several criteria:

- Minimal spam. The fewer outbound links a forum has, the better. Otherwise the effect of such links is simply diluted across thousands of other platforms.
- Maximum topical relevance. For example, for promoting a site offering construction services, the most effective forums or forum threads will be those dedicated specifically to construction. Aggressive spam in comments with links will only damage the promoted site and the business's reputation.
- Maximum usefulness. Substantive, genuinely helpful content always attracts more links and is more often approved by forum moderators.

In addition to forums, English-speaking markets use platforms such as quora.com, reddit.com, and similar sites for acquiring links. There, users can publish their own content, place links, and take part in discussions.

#### Rented links: what they are and why this method is outdated

For a long time, promoting sites with rented links was popular. Such links were usually placed chaotically in sidebars or footers of sites, with no topical relation to the page content.

In addition, rented links only stayed in place while a monthly fee was paid. Once links disappeared, their effect disappeared too, and everything had to be started from scratch.

In 2024 and beyond, we do not recommend using rented links for three reasons:

- they are placed on very low-quality sites;
- they can disappear;
- their effect is minimal, since usually many of them are placed from a single site.

### How to choose and buy links for site promotion correctly?

Google does not recommend buying links and emphasises that links should be earned naturally by creating high-quality content. This is genuinely important. But there is a flip side. Even very good content by itself generates few links. That process needs to be accelerated.

Any SEO work to grow link mass has its cost. So in a certain sense, all links are bought. How to buy links for site promotion correctly? We have already covered how to build article links. But it is also worth talking about how to choose donor sites you will get links from.

#### Donor parameters

Whether a donor site is suitable for placing a link can be judged by several criteria:

- Topic. The donor site should be as topically close to the promoted site as possible.
- Traffic. The higher the visitor numbers of the sites you want links from, the better. The point is not only that users can click through to your site. High donor traffic indicates that Google considers the site high-quality, and a link from such a site will give you the maximum lift in rankings and traffic.
- Geo. Another important factor is the region the site is associated with. This can be indicated by the domain name (for example, com.ua — Ukraine) or the main language of the content. To get organic traffic and positions in the region you need, prioritise donor sites tied to that same country.
- Domain Rating (DR) — a metric showing the relative strength of a site's link profile and its authority in a topic from the perspective of search engines. Popular SEO services such as Serpstat offer their own alternative metrics for the same purpose. The logic behind these parameters is similar, but the values may not match. In any case, the higher the DR of a site, the more impact a link from it will have.

#### Link parameters

You already know that the effectiveness of link-based promotion depends on the characteristics of the sites you get links from and the type of content (articles, forum threads, profile descriptions, comments). But that is not all. The links themselves also differ. Let us take a closer look.

#### Dofollow vs nofollow

Additional attributes are often added to links in code. They tell search bots how to behave when following the link. If there are no attributes, Google bots will follow the link and crawl your site's page. The nofollow attribute, added to a link, instructs bots not to pass link weight and other site metrics.

Does that mean nofollow links are useless? No. Google sees them, and our SEO experience shows that such links also influence ranking and can bring you traffic. Nofollow links also make a link profile look natural and reduce the risk of getting Google filters.

A logical question arises: what is the optimal ratio of nofollow to dofollow links? In professional circles you will encounter different opinions. Some say nofollow links should be the majority; others name specific percentages. We are confident the exact answer comes only from competitor analysis. You will often see sites in the top with nofollow links accounting for only a few percent of total link mass. The best decision is to be cautious and start with mostly nofollow links, then orient yourself by the indicators of competitors in the top.

#### Link anchor

The anchor is the text the link is attached to in the page code. It can tell Google for which query to rank the page at that address.

Anchors come in several types:

- Commercial. Words and phrases indicating that something is to be bought.
- Branded. Anchors mentioning a specific company.
- Generic (non-anchor). Phrases like "read here," "here," and similar in meaning.

Working with anchors incorrectly can create major problems for site promotion. Back in 2012, Google introduced the Penguin algorithm aimed at fighting spam and manipulation of search results. Violation risks the site disappearing fully or partially from results.

To avoid that, we recommend:

- not using commercial anchors with direct keyword matches;
- making the anchor list as varied as possible;
- using branded anchors where possible;
- using the maximum number of non-anchor links.

Non-anchor links are also links that appear not hidden behind text — for example: "the article can be found here yoursite.com/blog/article." Such links look natural to Google.

The percentage ratio of different anchor types and non-anchor links can vary. The answer to the question of what it should be lies in competitor analysis. But absolutely certainly, non-anchor links should make up the majority. Links with commercial anchors are better acquired carefully and on platforms with the highest SEO metrics.

#### Surrounding-anchor text

As the name suggests, this is the text surrounding the link anchor. It is important that it is:

- meaningful and useful for the audience;
- related to the topic of the promoted page and the site as a whole;
- in the same language as the promoted page;
- containing natural occurrences of other topical keywords.

You can also call the entire article written to acquire a link a piece of surrounding-anchor text.

How to write link-building articles correctly? Here are several important recommendations.

- Where possible, place the link in the first quarter of the text.
- Make the article as substantive and relevant as possible — add more facts and links to authoritative sources.
- Use screenshots, tables, and infographics where appropriate.
- If you use artificial intelligence to generate text, edit the article and check for factual and stylistic errors. This matters so that the text is actually read and the article page has good behavioural signals.
- Structure the article with paragraphs, subheadings, and lists.
- Use search queries in the text evenly and naturally.

Following the recommendations on anchor work, donor selection, and surrounding-anchor text will make link building safe and effective. Our advice will help you buy links for site promotion correctly.

### Summary. Evaluating the results of link-based promotion

You probably have a question. Is it possible to promote a site without links? Let us assume we are talking about not actively building links or buying them. In theory, a site can show positive dynamics even in that situation. But the growth will be so slow that the very existence of the site becomes unprofitable. So the optimal solution is active link-based promotion. With several important nuances:

- the right promotion strategy;
- choosing qualified specialists for SEO and link-building work;
- monitoring and correct evaluation of results.

The third point is worth covering in more detail. Results of link-based promotion are evaluated against several indicators:

1. Traffic to the site overall and to priority pages. If visits from search engines are growing, then link building is delivering results.
2. Positions in results for priority search queries. Be sure to identify search queries for the promoted pages in advance and track positions for them. This can be done with Ahrefs, Serpstat, and similar services. The faster the promoted pages grow in position for your key queries, the higher the overall promotion effectiveness.
3. Total number of search queries. This is not the main factor, but it is also significant. If the site is being indexed for an increasing number of search queries and positions for them are gradually rising too — you are on the right track.

If indicators remain unchanged or fall, the strategy needs to be adjusted — change the pace of link building, choose different ratios of link and anchor types.
