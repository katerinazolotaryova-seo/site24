## Body

### What is ChatGPT Search?

ChatGPT Search is a built-in tool that connects ChatGPT to the internet, letting it find and use the latest information from reliable sources in real time.

Unlike the standard model, which works more like an encyclopaedia drawing on knowledge from the past, ChatGPT Search is a synthesis of artificial intelligence and a search engine.

The ChatGPT Search feature is especially useful for finding up-to-date answers to questions about fast-changing events. Are you interested in sports scores, cryptocurrency prices, or the weather in Paris? Google is no longer the only option that can help.

### Key features of ChatGPT search

Traditional search engines offer a fixed set of tools for advanced search, but the choice of settings is quite limited and inconvenient. ChatGPT Search, by contrast, demonstrates impressive functionality and flexibility:

#### Natural conversation with the AI

ChatGPT search is conducted using natural phrasing. The user asks a question as they would ask a person and gets a clear answer in plain conversational form. Instead of a dry Google-style query like "Kyiv news today," you can carry on a relaxed dialogue:

#### Follow-up questions

The service lets users ask follow-up questions, and often initiates the continuation of the conversation itself. As a result, the search process is interactive and the answers received are thorough:

#### Cited sources

ChatGPT Search responses include clear references to authorship and sources. That lets you verify the information provided and conduct further research on the original websites.

#### Choice of answer format

Some responses can include visual elements — if that helps explain or show the point more clearly. The user can also specify the desired format for the information directly in the query: charts, bullet points, tables, and images:

#### Concise summaries

Instead of a long list of links as in a classic search engine, ChatGPT immediately formulates a concise and clear summary.

### How does the ChatGPT search engine from OpenAI work?

Let us look at the process in detail. From your question to the final answer, ChatGPT goes through a series of automated steps:

1. The user submits a query. For example: "What's new in Kyiv today?"
2. The model understands the meaning. It "reads" the query, breaks it into parts, and determines whether fresh information is required.
3. If it is — it goes to the internet. ChatGPT activates the search function.
4. It generates a query for Bing. The AI rephrases the question into a form that the search engine understands well. These can be phrases like: "Kyiv news June 15, 2025."
5. It receives a list of sites. The search engine selects the relevant links.
6. The AI reads the pages. It does not just collect headlines but "looks inside" the pages and analyses their content.
7. It picks out what is most useful. The AI extracts what matters and skips what does not.
8. It formulates the answer. You receive a short, human-readable summary without having to click through dozens of links.
9. It adds sources. Where needed, ChatGPT shows where the information came from — for verification or deeper study.

### Pros and cons of ChatGPT Search

The OpenAI AI-powered search engine has many advantages, but it is not free of drawbacks.

Let us start with the factors that will convince you to make ChatGPT Search your search engine of choice:

#### Ease of use

You do not have to be an expert. Even without knowledge of algorithms or operators, you will get a clear, concise, and relevant answer to any question.

#### Up-to-date data

The search function lets you get information literally a few minutes after publication. That is ideal for news, market quotes, and trends.

#### Context memory

The AI has good memory. If an earlier query was about an event in Kyiv, then a follow-up question about prices will not return the cost of groceries in Abu Dhabi supermarkets. That removes repetition and confusion.

#### Concise summaries instead of long lists

ChatGPT reads sites itself, extracts the gist, processes it, and provides a precise answer — quickly and logically. So instead of hundreds of links, you get one clear summary. That is far more efficient than clicking through 10 pages. Everything is on point — no fluff, no ads. If needed, you can clarify or expand the answer.

There is much to praise, but a few **weak points** are still holding ChatGPT Search back in the race for leadership:

#### Fabricated facts

When ChatGPT cannot find the right answer, it sometimes "makes one up" — and, to its credit, very convincingly. In critical fields such as medicine or law, that kind of improvisation can be too costly.

#### Loss of author voice

The AI retells the information it finds in its own words. Along with that, the author's tone or the brand's voice disappears. Instead of an authentic voice you get a uniform text without personality.

#### Apparent objectivity, hidden selectivity

GPT-based search appears to read the entire internet, but in reality it favours sources approved by the OpenAI team. Alternative or marginal but valuable opinions may simply not make it into the results.

#### Errors

Yes, the AI makes mistakes. Sometimes it is mundane grammatical errors, as in the case with "Kyiv" coverage, and sometimes it is a very significant distortion of fact. So every answer requires careful verification.

#### Nowhere to click

If the answer is already here, why open the site? This "zero-click" principle reduces traffic to original sources, which hurts publishers, bloggers, media, and the content economy at large.

### How to get featured in ChatGPT Search engines?

Now let us turn to the key question: how do you make the most of ChatGPT Search and adapt your content so the AI cites it more often? While there are no guaranteed recipes for success, since the technology is constantly evolving, the following practical tips are worth keeping in mind.

What you can do:

#### Publish on authoritative sources

OpenAI has agreements with several major media outlets, such as Reuters, AP, Vox, and Axel Springer. So if you are part of a large resource or have access to a platform with a strong reputation, that is a plus.

Tip: Publish on recognised platforms or build a partnership with them.

#### Structure content clearly and logically

ChatGPT extracts the essence from texts. Well-structured materials — with headings, subheadings, bullet lists, and short paragraphs — are easier for the model to "read."

> Tip: Follow the principles of UX writing. One paragraph — one idea.

#### Use clear formulations

The AI indexes texts with concrete formulations resembling user queries more effectively. For example:

"**What is Web3?**", "**5 ways to reduce business costs**", "**What is the difference between GPT-4 and GPT-3.5?**"

> Tip: Turn typical reader questions into the subheadings of your text.

#### Be an expert in the topic

The AI gives preference to material that demonstrates expertise (E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness). This is especially important for topics in the YMYL category (health, finance, safety, and so on).

> Tip: Show the author, their background, the data sources, and provide links to research.

#### Keep open access (no paywall)

Paywalled content is either invisible to the AI or only partially visible. That limits the chances of being included in the answer.

> Tip: Keep the main part of the text open — even if the rest is behind a subscription.

### Technical SEO for optimising for AI systems

From a technical SEO perspective, the following parameters matter for getting into AI systems:

1. Crawling and indexability.
   An XML sitemap, a correct robots.txt, and an absence of 4xx/5xx errors — that is the foundation, because AI Overview only draws on indexed pages.
   Canonical tags help avoid duplicate content and give Google clarity on what to show.

2. Loading speed and site performance.
   Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP/FID) are an important signal — both for ChatGPT ranking and for improving UX (user interaction).
   The use of a CDN, compression, CSS/JS inlining, and lazy loading meaningfully improves these indicators.

3. Mobile optimisation and responsive layout.
   Google AI Overviews are embedded into the mobile SERP, so the site must be mobile-friendly and render quickly on any device.

4. Security and accessibility.
   HTTPS is a required data-transfer protocol standard — for AI Overview, site security matters.
   Accessibility (ARIA, alt tags, contrast) — Google takes into account how accessible content is for all users.

5. Structured data (Schema.org).
   FAQ, HowTo, Article, Product, and other Schema.org structured data help the AI model recognise the key components of content and easily pull out the fragments it needs. JSON-LD is the most modern markup method to use.

6. Clear content structure and semantics.
   Clear H1–H3 hierarchy, short paragraphs, lists, and bullet points are the format the AI prefers.
   Breaking the site into content clusters strengthens semantic relationships and entity visibility.
