## Body

### Promoting medical websites: features and possible difficulties

Medical information affects people's lives and health. That is why Google sets special, stricter requirements for such sites — something you obviously need to factor into your SEO strategy. In this article we will look at the specifics of promoting sites in the medical niche and compile a list of parameters that absolutely must be addressed for successful SEO of a medical website.

### What kinds of sites exist in the medical niche?

Medical websites include:

- sites of any medical clinics, including dental;
- sites of beauty salons — especially those offering medical cosmetology;
- information sites about diseases, health, diets, and fitness;
- sites of testing labs and diagnostic centres;
- aggregators of medical information — doctors, clinics, medications;
- online pharmacies and sites selling medical products and equipment;
- sites of pharmaceutical companies and manufacturers — including vitamins and supplements;
- individual blogs on any of the topics above;
- official portals of public-health authorities and NGOs.

If a general-topic information resource has a medical section, the same requirements apply to that section as to medical sites.

### What Google's results page looks like

Google's results in the medical topic are quite varied.

Depending on the specific query, the top can include information sites alongside sites of clinics, medical centres, and labs offering services. The results also include aggregators such as [doc.ua](https://doc.ua/) and [helsi.me](https://helsi.me/), which gather information about clinics and specialists. Do not forget about the local pack on Google Maps, either.

To understand which type of page has a chance to reach the top, you need to study the current Google results for your target keywords first. If you see only articles there (or 70–80% informational pages), getting into the top 10 for that query with a commercial page is unlikely. In that case it is better to write a strong blog article that covers the topic more deeply and has a chance of "appealing" to Google.

### Defining the minimum site requirements

To understand the "minimum programme" of requirements for the site, we again study Google's top 10 for our priority queries. We pay special attention to:

- the total number of pages on the site;
- which categories and subcategories exist, and which services the site offers;
- the volume and quality of content on service pages;
- the information blocks that service pages consist of, and how the content is structured and visualised;
- whether there is a blog, the number and length of articles in it;
- the quantity and quality of backlinks (via Ahrefs).

Based on this data we build a development plan for our site. Some parts of the work can and should be entrusted to SEO specialists, but some can only be handled at the business level. For example, if you offer only 2–3 services and your top-10 competitors each have at least 70–80 services, then most likely it will be very hard to reach the desired Google top even with significant financial investment.

A mono-direction site can still get to the top in SEO, but only if it really becomes "the niche leader" in terms of content volume and quality, with strong external links.

### Important SEO specifics for medical sites

There are several key specifics in promoting a medical-services site:

1. Almost all medical-service types are highly competitive. That is why you need high site, content, and link quality to overtake competitors and reach the top.
2. The main high-volume queries in this niche are hard to classify cleanly as informational or commercial (for example, "treating sinusitis," "treating an ulcer"). A good solution can be creating a page in a "semi-landing" format — a symbiosis of an informational article and a service page.
3. Content published on medical sites must be high quality. Its creation has to involve not only a copywriter but also a medical expert who can cover the topic in depth and provide up-to-date data — not just rewrite already rewritten articles.
4. You must indicate the names of the authors of materials published on the site — both the journalist or editor and the doctor. The reputation of that specialist is also taken into account when ranking the material.
5. For quality ranking in Google, client reviews about both the clinic and the specialists are critically important. They are needed not only on the promoted site but also on third-party services.

### YMYL sites and EAT factors

Medical sites belong to YMYL (Your Money Your Life) sites, where it is important to pay attention to EAT factors during optimisation. We covered this topic in a separate article, which you can also read.

We will go over the main rules for medical topics in this article.

### Site structure requirements

Medical websites contain three types of pages:

- service pages;
- utility pages: Contacts, About Us, Certificates, and so on;
- blog articles.

Let us look at each type in detail.

#### Service page requirements

To create an ideal menu structure (a tree of services), you should first build the site's semantic core. But if you do not have resources for an SEO specialist or you need to launch the site "yesterday," you can look at competitors or follow the **"1 service = 1 page"** principle.

To design the structure of a specific landing page, it is also better to carefully study other top sites in your field and think about how to build a page that is even more convenient, informative, and useful for the user.

Or at minimum use our **checklist and add the following blocks** to the service page (below are general recommendations; in some niches specific sections may not apply, while specific others may need to be added):

1. A short (first-paragraph) and more detailed description of the service, including who it is for and what it is intended to do.
2. The advantages of the service itself compared to alternative ways of solving the problem.
3. The advantages of choosing your company for this procedure.
4. The price of the service — either an exact price list or "from – to" ranges.
5. Appointment hours and session durations.
6. A list of your specialists, with names, positions, photos, and a short summary of education and experience.
7. Treatment methods: the approach to treatment and a specific course of the procedure.
8. Equipment used for the procedure, with the manufacturer's name and photos of the medical office.
9. Recommendations for patients, including preparation and rehabilitation after the procedure, and other similar advice.
10. Indications and contraindications for the procedure.
11. Before-and-after demonstration of results, where possible.
12. Client reviews — with photos and names where possible.
13. Frequent patient questions and answers to them.
14. The ability to book an appointment directly from the service page — request forms, call-back buttons. It is even better if you have functionality for booking a specific time slot with a specific specialist.

#### Pricing page

Prices must be placed on each individual service page — it is an important factor both for ranking and for the client's decision-making.

It is also recommended to create an additional Price List (Pricing) page that collects prices for all services across all directions in one place.

It is important that such a page is conveniently structured — for example, with expandable lists or an internal search — so that finding the needed price is easy for the user.

#### Utility pages

The site should include pages such as: About the Clinic, Contacts, Reviews, Certificates and Licences, Promotions (if any), Doctors (more on which below).

Sometimes the list of such pages can be quite long.

The general rule: the more detailed personalised information about you is written on the site, the higher the chance of reaching and holding the Google top — and the more trust potential clients will feel.

And of course, any such information should be written as concretely and personally as possible — with dates, surnames, facts, photos, and addresses. Do not stop at general blurred phrases. To understand what to aim for, study competitors as well.

#### Information about doctors

For a medical website to rank highly, it is important to publish detailed information about the doctors or other medical experts. The content should be organised in a separate section as a catalogue listing, with individual pages for each doctor.

"Doctor cards" with a short description should also be pulled into the service pages.

The list of required information about doctors:

1. **Photo and full name.**
2. **Doctor qualifications:** it is important to publish information about qualifications — place of education, year of graduation, certificates and licences obtained, titles, and academic degree. It is also worth adding photos of certificates from continuing-education courses, information about participation in conferences, and other professional achievements.
3. **Work experience:** the site should contain information about doctors' professional experience, including overall time in medicine and experience in the particular field.
4. **Specialisation:** each doctor should have a clearly listed specialisation — for example, cardiologist, oncologist, or gynaecologist — together with a list of the services they provide.
5. **Scientific activity:** doctors involved in research can add information about publications in medical journals, scientific articles, and other works to demonstrate their expertise.
6. **Patient reviews:** publishing patient reviews about doctors can help build trust in the clinic and raise its rating.
7. **Photos and professional biographies:** it is recommended to publish photos of the doctors as well as their professional biographies so that patients can get a fuller picture of their doctors.
8. **Links to authoritative sources:** it is important to publish links to scientific and medical sources that confirm the doctors' expertise — for example, scientific articles or publications in medical journals.

#### Blog on a medical site

As we mentioned above, a blog on a medical site is essential — for many queries, the top is occupied by informational pages. That is why a blog can give us a lot of targeted traffic, which in this niche also converts well into service requests. In addition, having a strong topical blog signals to Google that the site is alive, that it is being worked on, and that it brings value to readers. As a result, positions improve for every query, including commercial ones.

What is worth writing in a medical blog:

- Information about diseases: causes, symptoms, treatment approaches, and specifics.
- Coverage of modern approaches and news in medicine.
- Clinical cases and "before-and-after" descriptions.

What should not be written:

- Unverified or controversial medical facts.
- Personal viewpoints that are not supported by the medical community.
- Recommendations for self-treatment.

You must indicate the authors, editors, and reviewers of every article — and one of them must be a medical specialist. It is also advisable to link to external authoritative sources on the topic.

For content authors, you should create separate landing pages with their professional credentials, achievements, and a list of publications — and include links to their professional social-media profiles (LinkedIn, Facebook).

### Building the external link profile

Even though the main specifics of promoting clinics and medical centres lie in on-page content optimisation, an article on this topic would be incomplete without external links.

As in any other topic, links matter. They must come from authoritative topical resources, from the same country as your site. The specific feature of the medical topic is that it is easy to obtain quality niche links — press releases, interviews with doctors, expert commentary, and articles in mass media.

#### Google Maps and Google My Business

[Original H3 in UA was a duplicated "Блог на медичному сайті" — replaced with a meaningful EN H3; see Notes above]

It is also important to have an optimised business card in the Google Maps service, since in this topic people often choose a clinic — among other things — by location.

Connect Google My Business — the platform that lets you manage your business card on Google Maps. You can update information about your company, respond to reviews, add photos, and much more.

**How to promote a company on Google Maps:**

1. Fill in complete information about your company. Make sure you indicate the correct address, phone, website, opening hours, and a company description. The more information you provide, the easier it will be for potential clients to find and contact you.
2. Pick the right category for your business card. The list of categories is available [here](https://www.propellic.com/blog/google-my-business-categories).
3. Request reviews. Reviews are an important part of optimising a business card on Google Maps. The more positive reviews your business has, the higher its rating and visibility.
4. Add photos. Photos help potential clients see and evaluate your business. Add photos of the clinic façade, the doctors, and the interior.
5. Use keywords in the description of your business. For example, if you run a dental office, make sure to mention keywords such as "dental treatment," "orthodontics," "teeth whitening," and so on.
6. Verify your information across other online services. Make sure information about your business is consistent across third-party online services such as Facebook and others. That will help improve your rating in Google Maps and elsewhere.
7. Publish posts in Google My Business. You can announce changes in the clinic's operations, news, and special offers for clients.

#### Presence in aggregators

You should also keep in mind that you can reach Google's top not only through your own site but also by being present in niche aggregators — for example, doc.ua or helsi.me. Before adding your business to each of them, review the rules for placing information and the ranking recommendations.

#### Reviews

For a clinic it is important to rank for the query "clinic name + reviews" and to get good ratings on third-party services. When potential clients search for a clinic online, they often look at reviews to determine how reliable and professional the clinic is.

You can find the list of relevant reviews by the same query "clinic name + reviews." To get good reviews there:

1. Ask your clients to write reviews. Do not hesitate to invite your clients to write a review about your clinic. Explain that the reviews will help others choose the best clinic.
2. Respond to reviews promptly. If you receive a negative review, reply to it and offer a way to solve the problem. That will show potential clients that you care about them and strive to improve the quality of your services.
3. Simplify the process of writing reviews. Make the process of writing a review as easy as possible for clients. For example, you can give them a link to the review page or place a QR code in the office.
4. Provide incentives for writing reviews. You can offer your clients a small discount or a gift for writing a review. That can motivate more clients to leave reviews about your clinic.
5. Take a responsible approach to reviews. Do not order them on specialised marketplaces. Such artificial reviews are usually visible, and that can damage your reputation.
