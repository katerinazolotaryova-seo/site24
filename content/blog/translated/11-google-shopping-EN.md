### Site requirements for Google Shopping setup

Before launching shopping ads for your online store, it is worth first making sure that the site meets Google's requirements and rules — and specifically the rules of the Merchant Center service, which lets you upload data about your products and store.

Considering the Google Merchant Center requirements, you should pay attention to the important points described below so that you can successfully launch shopping campaigns on Google Ads.

### 1. Contacts

One of the key aspects of preparing an online store for the launch of shopping campaigns in a Google Ads account is providing the business contact details.

Google requires that your site display sufficient contact information so that users can easily get in touch with you if needed. Usually it is enough to provide an email address, a phone number, and the physical address of your company.

In addition, if users cannot reach the people behind the site, this can create distrust and damage the company's image.

### 2. Order and payment terms

Order and payment terms are another important aspect to consider when preparing the site for launching shopping campaigns in a Google Ads account. If the terms are not spelled out, the user may simply not complete the purchase.

In line with the Google Merchant Center rules, your site must clearly spell out the order and payment terms. You need to publish the purchase rules — such as minimum quantity, delivery time, and order-cancellation rules.

In addition, the online store should offer different payment methods used in your country. In Ukraine these can be:

- cash on delivery;
- card payments;
- payment systems (for example, LiqPay, Privat24, Masterpass), and so on.

Providing proper information about contacts and the order and payment terms on your site means that users will be able to quickly and easily pick the option they want — and will be confident in the security of their purchases.

### 3. Delivery and return terms

This is an important item that must be covered on the online store's site. Users have to know what delivery and return terms apply to their orders. These include:

- delivery method;
- delivery regions;
- delivery cost.

You also need to indicate return terms, which can include time limits, rules for refunds, methods, and other details. Spelling out the delivery and return terms makes the purchase process more transparent and trustworthy for users — and improves their trust in your online store.

### 4. Product information and on-site checkout

Additional actions you need to take to prepare the site:

1. **The purchase process** — Make sure that buying on your site works correctly and does not glitch. This matters because any checkout issues can scare visitors away and cause sales losses.

2. **Rules for product presentation** — Make sure that every product on your site has proper-quality images and a full description, so that buyers can get all the information they need before purchasing. It is important to verify on the site that the price is current for every product.

3. **Match between products on the site and products in the data feed** — Before adding products to the data feed, make sure they exist on the site and match across all parameters listed in the feed. That means all product data in the feed must be accurate and aligned with the data on your site. If a product is no longer available on the site, it must be removed from the feed to avoid information mismatches.

### 5. User safety: SSL certificate and the HTTPS protocol

One of the most important aspects of preparing an online store for the launch of shopping campaigns in a Google Ads account is ensuring user safety.

User safety is a key aspect of the successful operation of any online store. Among other things, an SSL certificate must be installed on the site. This is a special type of certificate that ensures encryption of the information transmitted between your site's server and the user's browser.

When the SSL certificate is installed, all information transmitted from your server to the user's browser is encrypted using the HTTPS protocol. This ensures a secure information exchange between your site and the user's browser, and prevents potential attacks — such as MITM (man-in-the-middle) attacks, where an attacker can intercept and modify the transmitted information.

Failing to follow security requirements can lead to serious consequences, such as a leak of confidential user information and damage to your online store's reputation. In addition, you have to make sure the online store has sufficient protection against malware and malicious software. It is also worth checking your site's resilience against web hackers.

Using the HTTPS protocol is also reflected positively in Google Ads, since it signals that your site is secure.

### Registering a Google Merchant Center account

Google Merchant Center is a tool that lets you send your product data to Google. To start using it, you first need to create an account in it.

The registration process is fairly simple. You need to go to the Google Merchant Center site and click "Sign up." After that, you enter information about your business — name, address, contact details, and so on.

Once you have signed up, you need to verify your account. To do that, add a special code to your site or upload a verification file to your hosting.

In addition, to start using Google Merchant Center, you need to create a list of your products (a product feed) and upload it to the Merchant Center account. That, however, is a topic for a separate article.

### Allowed and prohibited products for advertising

Google has strict rules about which products may be advertised in Google Shopping campaigns. This is important to know, because violating the rules can result in your product being rejected for Google Shopping advertising — or in your Merchant Center account being blocked outright.

Examples of allowed product categories for advertising in Google Shopping:

- Apparel and accessories
- Electronics
- Beauty and health
- Food and beverages
- Home and garden
- Sporting goods
- Children's products
- Pet products

That said, it is important to note that some product categories may be restricted depending on the specific region or country, and Google Shopping may impose additional requirements on the products — for example, regarding labelling and certification.

The main categories of prohibited products include:

- **Prohibited or illegal goods:** such as drugs, weapons, pirated software, alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, and other similar products.

- **Goods that infringe copyright:** that contain counterfeit trademarks, copy someone else's products, or contain illegal content. For example, illegal software, fake licence keys, forged documents, music, or films. It is also not allowed to advertise the sale of unlocked keys (activation keys) for games or programs that do not belong to the advertiser.

- **Absence of physical product:** that is, products not physically in the seller's stock, products that have already been sold, or products that are no longer produced.

- **Parts or equipment for making weapons or other dangerous goods:** the sale of any parts is not allowed — such as bolts, sights, barrel housings, camouflage components, and so on.

- **Sale of any devices or equipment for producing drugs:** such as rings or tanks for cultivating mushrooms or cannabis.

- **Prohibited goods posing health and safety risks:** food products containing additives considered harmful, or cosmetics with dangerous chemicals.

- **Sale of products that may pose safety risks:** electrical appliances that do not meet safety standards; children's toys with sharp edges or dangerous metal parts.

- **It is prohibited to sell products that may be harmful to the environment:** such as certain chemicals that can pollute air, water, or soil.

- **Devices for altering or blocking signals:** such as GPS jammers and active radio stations without the required licences.

- **No services that violate the law are allowed:** for example, services for hacking computers or phones.

- **Goods containing personal information or data:** such as passwords, public keys, or credit-card numbers.

- **Pre-orders for products that have not yet been released:** with the exception of software and e-books.

- **Services and products containing discriminatory or offensive statements:** for example, selling products that promote racism or violence.

- **Misleading advertising:** products that make false or misleading claims. For example, claiming healing properties for a product when they are not supported by scientific research, or claiming features that are not actually present in the product.

- **It is not allowed to use manipulative methods** to increase clicks on the ad — for example, using titles or descriptions that have nothing to do with the advertised product, or creating a video that has nothing to do with the product in order to attract users.

- **Other products and services** that do not comply with Google Shopping rules, including products requiring a special licence or permit to sell.

- **Goods that infringe copyright:** such as counterfeit goods or selling assets from other games.

- **Absence of physical product.**

- **Products that do not meet quality or safety standards.**

Google's documentation contains a complete list of products that can and cannot be advertised in shopping campaigns. For detailed information about allowed and prohibited products, you can refer to:

- The list of prohibited products: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150006
- The list of products that may require additional verification: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6149970
- The list of advertising restrictions for specific countries: https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6150127

Please note that these lists can change periodically, so we recommend checking the latest version of the documentation on the Google Merchant Center site.

In addition, it is already known that Google plans to introduce new rules soon regarding advertising of products related to health, beauty, and financial services. The updates will primarily affect advertisers in the US and EU.

### Google Merchant Center account suspension

Google may suspend your account if you violate the rules for advertising products in Google Shopping. Here are some of the possible reasons for an account suspension:

- Illegal or prohibited products: if you are advertising products that violate laws or Google's rules.
- Incorrect product information: if information about your product is wrong or out of date.
- Insufficient product quality: if your products do not meet quality standards.
- Violation of user-safety rules: if your site contains malicious code or other threats to user security.

If your Merchant Center account is suspended, you will not be able to advertise products in Google Shopping. To get the account reinstated, you will need to fix the issues that led to the suspension and submit a review request to Google Support.

### How to reinstate a Google Merchant Center account

If your Merchant account is suspended, you can try to reinstate it by following these steps:

1. Check that you are not violating the rules for advertising products in Google Shopping.
2. Verify your Merchant Center account to make sure information about your products is accurate and up to date.
3. Fix the issues that led to the account suspension.
4. Submit an account review request to Google Support.

First of all, you need to understand the reason for the suspension. Usually Google sends an email describing the cause.

If the problem is related to a rule violation, you need to fix every violation and make sure your site meets the Google Merchant Center requirements.

The reason could also be related to suspicion of fraud or the use of malicious software — in which case you need to audit your site and remove all possible viruses. After that, you need to contact Google Support, submit a review request, and explain what was done to resolve the problem. This usually takes some time, so be prepared not to be able to launch your ad campaigns for a while.

The best way to avoid an account suspension is to follow the rules and requirements of Google Shopping and to keep your site protected from malware and other threats. You should also regularly check your account and site for violations so you can address them in time and avoid an account suspension.
